Where each screen comes from

Every surface above is owned by one story in Test-Flight-Improv/Voice-Video-Call-1to1-Feature/. The mockup follows the locked contracts (L1–L7) — the 45 s offer TTL, the single call_push_request ring mechanism, and the transport pill states.

VC-04

Signaling envelopes

The rail shows the real protocol: call_offer (with callId + 45 s TTL), call_answer, call_ice_candidates, call_end / _decline / _busy. Fast-path only — never store-and-forwarded, so a stale offer can't ring anyone 20 minutes late.

VC-05

Foreground audio call

Outgoing, incoming and active-call screens with mute, speaker and hang-up on a glass control bar. The transport pill reports the live ICE pair: direct, punched or TURN relay, plus RTT from getStats.

VC-06

Android ring path

A killed phone rings via a high-priority FCM data push → full-screen intent (gallery below). Ring budget ≤ 3.0 s p95; the ring never outlives the offer: timeout = min(45 s, TTL remaining).

VC-07

iOS CallKit

Same call, Apple rules: PushKit voip push must report to CallKit immediately — the native ringing UI (gallery below). Adds the missing voip/audio background modes, test-locked.

VC-08

Video & mid-call upgrade

Video-from-start or upgrade during audio — the callee always consents before their camera turns on. Local preview PiP, camera flip, speaker default on.

VC-09

Reliability & quality

Wi-Fi drop mid-call → ICE restart with a visible reconnecting state (≤ 10 s budget). Every call ends with a quality trail — setup time, path, RTT, loss — and a privacy toggle forces TURN so your IP never meets the peer.
