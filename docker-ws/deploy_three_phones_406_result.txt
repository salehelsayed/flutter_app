=== incident: the wait loop hung on its own busy check ===
The iOS build finished at 21:08Z but deploy_406_chain.sh spun until 21:18Z.
docker-ws/ios_build_progress.sh matched "dart" when reporting build processes,
and five long-lived dartvm processes from earlier test runs (etime 1h31m) under
~/development/flutter-3.47.2 kept the busy count above zero forever. The
pattern now matches only real compile/link work
(xcodebuild|swift-frontend|clang|ld$|Xcode.app).

Lesson: a liveness probe must match the WORK, not the toolchain. A test or
analyzer process living under the same SDK looks identical to a build unless
the pattern names the compiler.

Second lesson (repeat of a known landmine): `pkill -f <pattern>` kills the
issuing shell when the pattern appears in its own command line. Resolve pids
with ps/awk and kill those instead.
