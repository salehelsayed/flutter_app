1. SE-43: fully offline recipient and expiry (diagnosis only).
1.1 Start with one working accepted-contact call; verify two-way audio, a terminal history row, and a background missed card control. Current client default signal lifetime is 40 seconds under a 45-second ceiling; later messages have their own lifetimes. The initial invite deadline does not bound every later artifact.
1.2 Disconnect the callee completely from all networks, including any still-connected path. In separate runs the caller cancels and lets the call go unanswered. Keep peers/initial states fixed.
1.3 Remain offline past every actual invite/terminal expiry, then repeat with minutes and a longer offline window. Reconnect in foreground and background separately. Observe history and local cards separately: foreground suppression of a card does not prove absent history.
1.4 Relaunch the callee and repeat reconnect. Expected safe behavior: no expired ring, no late Answer, no duplicate terminal row/card. Record whether missing history is reproduced. Do not turn this finding into a catch-up or TTL change.
1.5 Control: reconnect while the caller and authenticated invitation remain valid. Record eligible ringing, answer, terminal history, and two-way audio separately. Compare with the expired runs.
Source interpretation: relay terminalization removes deliverable mailbox events and keeps a bounded replay tombstone; it does not create an authenticated missed-call outcome for a recipient that received nothing. A later terminal envelope can outlive the invitation, but the codec rejects that envelope after its own expiry. Headless history requires a received authenticated terminal. “History only” cannot supply an event that never arrived. Device reproduction remains NOT RUN.
