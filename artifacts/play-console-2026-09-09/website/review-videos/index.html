Move account to a new phone

Foreground service type: DATA_SYNC

The user selects Move from old phone, confirms the pairing code, starts the account transfer, and backgrounds the old phone. Android displays Moving account while progress advances. Captured confirmation screens show Transfer complete and the migrated @pixel account on the new device.
