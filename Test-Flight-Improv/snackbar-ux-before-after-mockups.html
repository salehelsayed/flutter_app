The thread: one ephemeral, screen-global, ~4-second snackbar is doing eight different jobs — message failures, media errors, permission gates, lifecycle changes, destructive admin actions, invite outcomes, quiet successes. Each plan moves the feedback onto the surface that fits its lifetime and binds it to the object it's about.
