QR enters the Orbit

My QR and Scan are leaving the all-chats header and need a home on the inner-circle surface. Four placements below — every frame reproduces the shipped screen: ambient glows, the 193 view toggle, the create button, dashed rings at r62/r108, and the 194 unread satellites.

Option A safest ship
The Dock

The two pills move down as they are, docked just above the Feed–Orbit pill where the thumb already lives.

Why it works
  • Zero new vocabulary — ships the existing _PillButton untouched.
  • Bottom-center is the easiest reach on a 6.1″ phone.
  • Reads as one action zone together with the nav pill.
Watch out
  • Two stacked floating bars crowd the bottom and compete for attention.
  • On short screens the dock squeezes the empty-state hint.

Option B
Quiet Chrome

QR and Scan join the top strip as twin 40 px circles — the same chrome species as the view toggle and the create button.

Why it works
  • The top-center strip is empty; utilities already live on this row.
  • Perfectly consistent chrome: surfaceSubtle fill, hairline border, 20 px glyph.
  • The orbit itself stays untouched — ceremony preserved.
Watch out
  • Worst thumb reach; four circles start to read as a toolbar.
  • No labels — the two glyphs must carry meaning alone.

Option C design pick
Ring Satellites

Grow-your-circle actions ride the outer ring itself — two glass satellites docked on the bottom arc, in the vocabulary the +N overflow badge already established.

Why it works
  • Strongest conceptual fit: scanning someone adds them to this orbit, so the entry point sits where new friends will appear.
  • The vocabulary exists — glass circle + dashed border already means "an object on the ring that isn't a person".
  • Green glyph + tiny label anchor meaning without breaking the constellation.
Watch out
  • Occupies 2 of 8 outer slots (13 → 11 visible friends before the +N badge).
  • Fixed geometry must dodge the overflow-badge slot.
outer-ring slots 3+4θ = 60° / 105°overflow-badge glass
Option D
Identity Hub

Your QR is you — it lives as a badge on your avatar at the center of the orbit. Scan becomes the single outward action beneath the caption.

Why it works
  • Semantically exact for My QR: the center node is your identity.
  • Halves the button count on a ceremonial surface.
  • The scan pill reuses the existing string "Scan a friend's code".
Watch out
  • My QR discoverability drops to an 18 px badge — likely needs a first-run hint.
  • Splits a pair users learned together; center-avatar tap gains a new meaning.

recommendation

C for the product, A for the calendar

Ring Satellites is the only option that makes the migration feel designed rather than relocated — the actions that grow the orbit live on the orbit, using glass-on-ring vocabulary the overflow badge already shipped. If it needs to land this week with zero visual review risk, The Dock moves the exact existing pills and touches nothing else. B and D are documented as considered-and-declined: B taxes the quiet top strip for the worst reach zone, D trades too much discoverability for elegance.

Destinations unchanged Both actions keep their full-screen pushes — QRDisplayWired (white QR card, green glow) and QRScannerWired (camera + green corner markers). No sheets introduced.
What remains behind After migration the all-chats header keeps only the "Close Friends" title; the search-active pill-hiding rule disappears with the pills.
Untouched chrome Every option keeps the 193 view toggle (top-left, physical) and the create button (top-right) exactly where they are.
Color note The pill accent #157A39 was tuned for the list surface. On the pure-black orbit canvas consider brightening icon/label to #1DB954 — flagged, not applied, in A and D.
