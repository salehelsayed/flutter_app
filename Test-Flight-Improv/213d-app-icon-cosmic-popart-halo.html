Halo × cosmic pop-art 🎈

Borrowed from your inspiration mural: hand-drawn swirling galaxy ribbons in purples and magentas, marbled balloon-planets with contour line-work, chunky flat cartoon stars in golden yellow, bold outlines, deep indigo sky. The Halo geometry you picked stays — one ring, a core, a friend with an unread dot — but each variant pulls a different element of that art style into the icon.

Five pop-art halo directions

P1 · GALAXY SWIRL

Galaxy Swirl

The mural's swirling purple galaxy ribbons flow behind the teal halo, with chunky golden stars and a little cratered moon-friend. The most literal translation of the inspiration — and it keeps the brand-teal ring.

P2 · BALLOON PLANET

Balloon Planet

The friend on the ring becomes one of the mural's marbled balloon-planets — golden with swirl contour bands, thick indigo outline, white highlight — floating on a lavender hand-drawn halo.

P3 · WHIRLPOOL

Whirlpool

The whole icon becomes the mural's spiral galaxy: hand-wobbled purple bands with one hot-pink streak spiraling into the glowing core — and the friend is a chunky yellow star riding the swirl. Boldest of the five.

P4 · COMIC TEAL

Comic Teal

The gentlest adoption: the current teal halo redrawn like a comic cel — dark under-outline, offset highlight stroke, outlined core and friend — with one purple swirl, one pink streak, and cartoon stars.

P5 · MAGENTA BLOOM

Magenta Bloom

A nod to the astronaut's rose: the core becomes a marbled magenta planet-bloom inside a white hand-drawn halo, glowing pink, with lavender swirls and a yellow-dotted moon-friend.

Same five, mixed in red · green · white · black

Q1 · GALAXY SWIRL

Galaxy Swirl

Emerald ribbon-swirls over green-black, one crimson streak up top, white hand-drawn halo and chunky white stars (one red). The mural's energy, your four colors.

Q2 · BALLOON PLANET

Balloon Planet

The marbled balloon-planet goes crimson — dark-red swirl bands, ink outline, white highlight — on a mint hand-drawn ring with a green unread dot.

Q3 · WHIRLPOOL

Whirlpool

The spiral galaxy in greens — forest to emerald to mint spiraling into the white core, with one crimson band and a white cartoon-star friend wearing a red dot.

Q4 · COMIC INK

Comic Ink

The comic-cel halo in emerald with a near-black ink under-outline, a crimson ribbon sweeping the corner, a green streak below, and ink-outlined core, friend, and red dot.

Q5 · CRIMSON BLOOM

Crimson Bloom

The rose nod in your palette: a marbled crimson planet-bloom glowing red inside a white halo, on green-black with mint swirls and a green-dotted moon-friend.
