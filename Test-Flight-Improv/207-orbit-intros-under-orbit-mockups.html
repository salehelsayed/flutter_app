Where should Intro live under Orbit?

The red badge on the Orbit nav button counts pending introductions — but the default Orbit view (the Inner-Circle rings) renders nothing about them. The review surface hides on the all-chats list view, behind the top-left toggle. This exploration moves the Intro affordance onto the default orbit surface: four placements, one recommendation. Sending an intro (from the 1:1 chat) is untouched — this is only about seeing and reviewing pending intros.

Today: the badge points at a blank room

Pending intros produce a red “3” on the Orbit nav button — yet landing on Orbit shows only the rings. Nothing explains the badge.

  • The Intros tab + green banner exist only on the all-chats list view (orbit_screen.dart:810) — a surface reached via the top-left view toggle, which most users never flip.
  • The Feed shows only completed intros (handshake letters); pending Accept/Pass is invisible there.
  • The “New Introduction” notification deep-links into this hidden tab — tap the notification, land somewhere you’ve never seen.
  • What already works: the review UI itself (IntroRow cards, Accept/Pass, “From {introducer}” sections) is solid — it’s the entrance that’s lost.

Option A design pick
Intro dock — under the rings

A compact green capsule floats in the empty band directly beneath the orbit canvas: the pending people’s own faces, a count, one tap to review. Literally “Intro, under Orbit.”

Why it works
  • Occupies the only reliably free region of the inner-circle view — between the canvas bottom (≈y590) and the find pill (≈y696). Collides with nothing.
  • Intro identity reads instantly: green vocabulary + dashed rings = “not yet in your orbit.”
  • Avatar faces create a curiosity pull a generic banner never will — these are real people waiting.
  • Tap reuses today’s banner wiring (onIntroBannerTap → Intros review); zero new review UI to ship.
Watch out
  • Must hide during sculpt-edit, like all orbit chrome.
  • Overflow arcs expand downward into this band — the dock should fade while arcs are open.
  • Strictly count-gated: renders nothing at 0 pending.

Waiting orbit — ghost nodes

Pending intros appear as dashed ghost avatars parked on a waiting arc just outside Ring 2, at the bottom of the canvas — people literally waiting to enter your orbit. Tap a ghost to accept or pass.

Why it works
  • Zero new chrome — the metaphor is the product. The strongest emotional read of the four.
  • Desaturated + dashed + pulsing dot cleanly separates “almost in” from real contacts.
  • Scales 1–3 pending gracefully along the arc.
Watch out
  • The canvas already hosts groups, overflow “+N” arcs, unread satellites and sculpt handles — tap ambiguity with real nodes is a genuine risk.
  • Expanded overflow arcs occupy exactly this space; the waiting arc must yield or fold.
  • >3 pending needs its own “+N” fold; per-ghost Accept/Pass still needs a popover surface anyway.

The ignore path: quiet by default, loud only on news

Not every intro deserves an answer — and Pass is not a quiet escape: passing sends a notification to the introducer (pass_introduction_use_case.dart:12-13). Users who’d rather not reject anyone will simply ignore, and an ignored intro lives for 30 days before auto-expiring (introduction_model.dart:138). Whichever option ships must respect ignoring as a first-class choice:

  • Dismissal demotes, it doesn’t delete: swipe the dock away and it collapses into a calm remnant — a 32px dashed chip, monochrome, no count — the same “folded content” vocabulary as the rings’ “+N” overflow badge. Furniture, not notification.
  • Changed your mind? One tap back: tap the remnant any time and the review reopens — no waiting for news, no hunting through the view toggle. (An Undo snackbar also covers accidental swipes.)
  • Badge counts unseen, not pending: dismissing clears the red nav badge too. Pending-but-seen intros also stay listed in the Intros tab — the always-on fallback surface.
  • Only news re-inflates it: a genuinely new intro brings the full dock back at the size of the news (“1 new introduction”), not the backlog.
  • Total silence stays available: long-press the remnant → hidden entirely for the current set (persisted like the chat IntroBanner’s “Maybe later”, dismissIntroBanner / migration 020); recall then lives in the Intros tab until expiry does the cleanup after 30 days.
  • Per option: A/C collapse to this same remnant; D’s handle effectively is the remnant already — it just sheds its count when seen; B is the cautionary tale — faces parked on the canvas have no graceful quiet state, one more reason B stays declined.

recommendation

A now — D when the tray earns it

Ship Option A. It puts intros literally under the orbit, in the one band nothing else claims, and uses the pending people’s own faces as the affordance. Its tap can reuse the existing banner jump to the Intros review list (onIntroBannerTap wiring), so the first ship is one small component with the full discoverability payoff. Pair it with the ignore path above — dismissing collapses the dock to a calm dashed remnant (tap to come back any time, long-press for total silence), badge = unseen, 30-day expiry — so a user who never wants to answer never feels nagged, and one who changes their mind is one tap away. When in-place review is worth the build, Option D’s tray slots in behind A’s dock unchanged — the dock becomes the handle, the tap opens the tray instead of switching views. C works but crowds the busiest strip of the screen, forces the find pill to move, and double-mounts a component that already struggles for attention. B is the most charming and the most dangerous — the canvas already juggles groups, overflow arcs, unread satellites and sculpt handles; parking tappable people among them buys delight at the price of ambiguity. Keep B as a later delighter once the canvas census calms down.

Count & gating

Every option is driven by reviewCount = folded pending intros + pending group invites (feed_wired.dart:541-581) — the same number as the red nav badge. At 0, nothing renders; with the ignore path, dock and badge key off unseen rather than raw pending.

Edit & find etiquette

Sculpt-edit hides all orbit chrome — the new element hides too. The find pill + chip strip own y≥660; A/B stay clear of it, C lifts the pill (armed-edit reflow precedent).

Sending is untouched

Intros still start from the 1:1 chat (overflow menu + IntroBanner card). This decision only relocates the receive/review affordance onto the default orbit view.

Deep links follow

The “New Introduction” notification currently lands on the hidden Intros tab. Whichever option ships becomes its target; the all-chats Intros tab remains the full management surface.
