A blocked exit should explain the next move.

A sole admin cannot leave an active group. Say why at the moment they try, then offer the two valid paths: choose another admin or dissolve the group for everyone.

01 · Proposed flow

Explain the rule where the action stops.

Rename the active-group action to Leave. If the person is the only admin, replace the generic destructive confirmation with a persistent choice sheet that can take them directly to recovery.

02 · Two outcomes

Two paths, two clear consequences.

The sheet starts from the user’s intention—remove the group from this phone—then makes the group-wide consequence explicit before any destructive action.

You’re the only admin The active group cannot be left without an admin.
Choose another admin → LeaveYou leave and the history is removed from this phone. The group continues for everyone else.
GROUP CONTINUES
Dissolve for everyone → Optional local deleteMessaging ends for every member. Read-only history remains until each person removes it locally.
GROUP ENDS

Name the real action

Active-group removal begins with leaving. The swipe label should say that before the user taps.

Explain the safeguard

“Only admin” makes the restriction predictable. “Something failed” does not.

Put recovery in place

The explanation stays visible and links directly to choosing another admin or dissolving.

Separate the scopes

Say plainly whether an action affects this person, this phone, or everyone in the group.
