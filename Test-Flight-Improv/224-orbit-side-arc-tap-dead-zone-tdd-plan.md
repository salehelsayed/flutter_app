## Exact Problem Statement
On the Orbit inner-circle screen with the overflow arcs expanded, avatars seated on the left/right flanks of an arc are painted on screen but do **not** respond to taps — the chat never opens. Avatars near the top of the arcs, and all ring avatars, open chats normally. The user hit this after enlarging avatars (`avatarScale`) and widening the arcs (`orbitGap`/`arcWrap`) via the 198 sculpt knobs, but the dead zone also exists at **default** knobs once a second arc appears (arc 1 seats are pinned at |dx| = 170 by the pinch bound).

The tap is not merely lost: it falls through to the opaque background gesture layer behind the canvas, so a "tap the avatar" gesture is interpreted as tap-away (closes find, exits edit).

What must improve: every rendered arc seat whose tap box the geometry engine places within the phone's usable width must be tappable, in LTR and RTL, at any in-range knob combination.
What must stay unchanged (→ preserved-green sentinels): all seat *positions* (geometry math byte-identical — the domain suite locks the pinch bound at |dx| ≤ 170.5), collapsed-canvas 320px geometry incl. 205 vertical centering, planted-circle behavior during sculpt drags, same-frame handle seating (TC-198F family), vertical overhang behavior (223), badge/label/self-avatar anchoring, background long-press/double-tap gestures.

## Root Cause (verify → refute confirmed)
The viz canvas is a fixed-width box: `SizedBox(width: _size /*320*/, height: boxHeight)` wrapping `Stack(clipBehavior: Clip.none)` — `orbital_visualization.dart:326-335`, constants `orbit_arc_layout.dart:21-22` (`kOrbitCanvasSize=320`, center 160). The geometry engine deliberately seats arcs out to `|dx| = kOrbitPinchHalfWidth = 170` (`orbit_arc_layout.dart:40`, pinch at `:186-192`) and past it toward `|dx| ≈ r` when `arcWrap > 1.6` releases φ toward 2.9. Vertical overflow grows the box (`orbitArcOverhang` `:228-246` top, `orbitArcBottomOverhang` `:252-267` bottom → `boxHeight`/`cy` at viz `:150-152`), but **no horizontal equivalent exists**: `cx` is pinned to 160 and the box width never grows. `Clip.none` affects paint only — `RenderBox.hitTest` rejects any position outside the 320-wide box, so a seat with tap-center `|dx| > 160` is visible but unreachable; the tap lands on the opaque background `GestureDetector` (`inner_circle_interactive_surface.dart:653-662`), a *sibling behind* the viz.

Probe proof on HEAD (temporary widget test, deleted): geometry av=1.4/og=1.5 (both in-range, `orbit_geometry_prefs.dart:40-58`), 20 friends expanded → edge seat idx13 at dx=−167.97 painted on-screen at (232.0, 191.2); `tester.tap` at its center fired **no** `onFriendTap`; top seat idx16 (dx=0) fired. Max tap-box overhang past the box edge: 31.97px per side.

Adversarial verify→refute (workflow `wf_e2a9b4eb-f9f`), both lenses CONFIRMED:
- **No production escape hatch**: `_onBackgroundTap` is end-edit/close-find only (surface `:346-354`), no nearest-seat router; `OrbitalAvatar`'s Semantics wraps a plain opaque GestureDetector (touch rides normal hit-testing, `orbital_avatar.dart:169-179`); no Transform/OverflowBox trick; host chain (OrbitWired → OrbitScreen → SafeArea → surface) cannot widen a fixed-width SizedBox. On a 390pt phone the padded content width is 342 ≥ 320, so the canvas is NOT clamped on HEAD — the boundary is exactly |dx| > 160, identical to the probe.
- **All alternative causes eliminated**: no overlay covers the side seats (toggle/search/pill/dock/find/edit chrome all elsewhere or gated); avatar entrance settles at identity and Opacity never blocks hits; labels sit under `IgnorePointer` (viz `:444`); dim layers are pure Opacity; unread satellite is `IgnorePointer`+`ExcludeSemantics`.

Refuted / do-NOT-re-introduce:
1. "Background tap could route to the nearest seat" — there is no router on HEAD and adding one is NOT the fix (it would fire ghost-taps on genuine tap-away). Fix the hit-test bound instead.
2. "The scroll-view padding clamps the canvas on HEAD" — false (342 ≥ 320). The padding matters only AFTER the box grows (it becomes the clamp) — it is a fix hazard, not the HEAD cause.
3. "Clamp seat |dx| to the canvas" — forbidden; the domain suite deliberately locks seats out to |dx| ≤ 170.5 (`orbit_arc_layout_test.dart:96-106`). The box must grow to the seats, never the seats shrink to the box.
4. A11y note (no action): SemanticsAction.tap likely already works out-of-box (semantics rects are unclipped) — assistive-tech only; unchanged by the fix.
