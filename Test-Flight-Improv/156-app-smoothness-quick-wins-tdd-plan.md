## Exact Problem Statement
Installed from TestFlight / Google Play, the app feels heavy: screen-to-screen navigation hitches, scrolling never feels glassy, and the device runs warm at idle. The audit traced this to (a) the raster thread never resting — a per-bubble Gaussian blur and an always-animating ambient background repaint every frame even at rest — and (b) chats/feed redoing O(N) work per interaction (oversized avatar decodes, missing DB indices, an unconditional attachments read per incoming message, per-row formatter/lookup churn, an entrance animation on every initial row, and a launch-time re-scan of two encrypted tables).

**What must improve:** remove the steady-state raster cost (blur, idle animation, repaint-bleed), stop oversized image decodes, give the two hot list queries an index, eliminate per-interaction redundant work, and stop re-scanning encrypted tables on every cold start — all WITHOUT changing visible content, message ordering, reliability, or migration data.

**What must stay unchanged (→ preserved-green sentinels):** message text/ordering/grouping, quote resolution results, transport-status glyphs, the 131-class notif-tap incoming-message surfacing (KEEP both `incomingMessageStream` AND `messageChanges`), feed reduce-motion behavior, all existing migrations' data, and the cosmic/mirrored/daylight background variants.

**Two production orbit animations MUST keep animating — do NOT touch them (user-required, 2026-06-24):**
1. **Friend-profile orbit** — tapping a friend's avatar opens `contact_profile_screen.dart` ("orbital dossier"), which rotates orbit rings behind the centred `UserAvatar` via its **own** `_orbit` `AnimationController(..repeat())` (`:77-80`, rotating field `:242`, avatar `:276`). Reached from `orbit_wired.dart:1857` and `conversation_wired.dart:4129`.
2. **First-open orbit** — the onboarding screen (`first_time_experience_screen.dart`) shows `EmptyCircleState` (`empty_circle_state.dart`) at the bottom, animated by its **own** `_controller(..repeat())` (`:18-27`) inside `TickerMode(enabled: true)` (`first_time_experience_screen.dart:205`).

Both are driven by **dedicated controllers that do NOT read `MediaQuery.disableAnimations`** (verified — neither file references it). They are therefore **outside** QW-3's reach: QW-3 only adds reduce-motion gating to `ambient_background.dart`'s `_shouldAnimate`. These orbits must animate exactly as today (including when Reduce-Motion is on — that is current, user-desired behavior). They are also separate from QW-13's experimental tabs (the `kOrbit2/3PrototypeEnabled` flags live in `lib/features/orbit2|orbit3/`, not in `contact_profile`/`empty_circle`/production `lib/features/orbit/`). → pinned by **TC-24** (friend-profile orbit) and **TC-25** (first-open orbit).
