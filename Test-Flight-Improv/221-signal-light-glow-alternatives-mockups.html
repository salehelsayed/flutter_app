Five ways to make the light orbit stop washing out.

You like a light home ground — but on the shipped Signal theme the halos around your avatar and contacts, and the dashed rings, go pale and hazy on the near-white ground. The cause is the primitive: a translucent soft-blur bloom only reads as light over a dark backdrop; over white it just smudges. Below is the current look, then five complete light treatments — each kills the pale-glow with a different primitive. Every text tone is checked to WCAG AA.

CAUSE
Bloom on white. Node halo = soft BoxShadow (blur size·0.45) at 45–50% alpha — no dark backdrop to glow against.
CAUSE
Ghost rings. Orbit rings are 1.5px dashes at 28–38% alpha — they nearly evaporate.
CAUSE
Flat void. A dead-flat porcelain ground gives the composition nothing to hold onto.
FIX
Change the primitive. Crisp ring · filled disc · tinted ground · sonar frame · tight bloom — pick your taste.
One phone template · re-themed entirely through CSS custom properties + one node-mode per palette.
Semantics preserved on every ground — self = violet, contact = red/coral, online = green, accent (＋ / active nav) = electric violet.
Geometry references orbital_ring_painter.dart & orbital_visualization.dart (_OrbitNodeHalo). Contrast ratios stated per palette.
