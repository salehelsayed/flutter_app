=== why the badge kept looking absent all session ===

opening a conversation is precisely what clears the call unread. The app also
restores into the last conversation on relaunch, so even a restart clears it
before Orbit can be inspected.

To see it on a device: miss a call, do NOT open that chat, and view the ring.
