Icon color studies — Black · Red · White · Green

The same six Orbit-era designs, re-dressed in the four requested colors. Each design gets two treatments (one luminous-dark, one bright) so black never reads as a dead black square — every dark variant carries a glow. Shades are tuned to sit well together: emerald and flag greens, a warm crimson, ivory whites, green-tinted blacks.
