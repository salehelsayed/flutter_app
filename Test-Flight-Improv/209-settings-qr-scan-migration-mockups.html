The QR comes home to Settings

Since 206 the orbit's center avatar opens Settings — the page is now the identity surface. So My QR and Scan leave the orbit's top chrome and move under the profile picture and nickname. Four takes below: each migrates the pair, and each answers "improve the settings page" at a different amplitude — from a one-row diff to a full regroup.

Today → orbit top chrome
The pair (196 Option B) sits between the view toggle and the create button — icon-only, worst thumb reach, and invisible from Settings, the page that owns identity.
Option A safest ship
Twin Pills

The chrome pair lands as two labeled pills directly under the nickname. Nothing else on the page moves.

Why it works
  • One-widget diff: SettingsProfileSection gains a Row; every other card ships untouched.
  • The icons finally get their labels back — on Orbit they were Semantics-only.
  • Green-tinted pills read as "actions", distinct from the neutral setting cards below.
Watch out
  • The page stays a long undifferentiated card stack (~2.5 screens of scroll).
  • Peer ID still floats alone one card below the identity it belongs to.

Option B design pick
Identity Card

Everything that is you — avatar, name, peer ID, QR — fuses into one glass card; the page below gains named sections.

Why it works
  • Semantically exact: the QR encodes peerId + key + name, so it lives with them (the WhatsApp/Signal-proven pattern).
  • The 44 px QR thumbnail beside the peer ID teaches "your ID is scannable" before any button is pressed — and tap-throughs to the full code.
  • Section labels (APPEARANCE · MEDIA · PRIVACY · ACCOUNT) give the long page a spine without rebuilding any control.
Watch out
  • Tallest identity block: ~300 px before the first actual setting.
  • Three QR affordances in one card (thumb, button, scan) — the thumbnail and button must open the same screen.
merges SettingsPeerIdCardqr thumb 44pxcontrols unchanged, labels added
Option C biggest cleanup
One Screen

Always-expanded control cards compress into grouped rows with values; the whole page fits with no scroll, and QR / Scan become the two biggest targets on it.

Why it works
  • The entire settings surface is visible at once — rows read like an index, values (Default, Compressed…) answer without opening.
  • Each row opens a focused sub-sheet (background chooser, quality picker, recovery phrase) instead of stacking every control inline.
  • Nearby stays an inline switch — the one-tap toggle keeps its one tap.
Watch out
  • Biggest build: 5 sub-sheets plus row state; every settings widget is touched.
  • Background/quality changes become two taps instead of one.

Option D brand flourish
Personal Orbit

Your avatar gets its own dashed ring, and My QR + Scan dock on it as satellites — the exact glass-on-ring vocabulary the orbit already taught.

Why it works
  • Strongest continuity: satellite = "object on a ring that isn't a person", straight from the 196-C exploration.
  • Identity actions literally orbit your identity; the ceremonial arrival matches the 420 ms slide-up route.
  • Pairs with C's grouped rows below, so the flourish doesn't cost the cleanup.
Watch out
  • The ring hero spends ~210 px of a utilitarian scrolling surface on ceremony.
  • 40 px satellites sit near the camera badge — three small round targets in one cluster.

recommendation

B's head on C's body — A if it must ship this week

Identity Card is the pick for the migration itself: the QR joins the peer ID and name it encodes, and the page gains a spine of named sections while every existing control ships untouched. When the page earns a real cleanup, One Screen is the end-state — and the two compose (B's card sitting on C's rows). Twin Pills is the calendar option: one Row in the profile section, nothing else moves. Personal Orbit is documented as considered-and-declined for this surface — the ring is beautiful continuity, but ceremony taxes a page people visit to flip a switch; spend it where the orbit already is.

Scanner is the heavy half QRScannerWired takes ~30 deps mostly to rebuild a fresh FeedWired after the success dialog (qr_scanner_wired.dart:384-434). Hosted in Settings, prefer: pop back + appShellController.switchTo(feed) — shrinks the bundle instead of forwarding it.
Orbit still hears about scans Orbit's .then(_applyRouteChanges) refresh dies with the entry point (orbit_wired.dart:2341). It already listens to externalRouteChangesListenable (:2253) — route scan results through that.
Migration QRs come free The scanner's account-migration branch needs the Move-account deps — which SettingsWired already owns for "Start move" (:507-530). Settings is the natural host.
What retires OrbitQrChromeButtons + its mount (orbit_screen.dart:602); keys orbit-my-qr-button/orbit-scan-button re-key to settings-*; dead pre-196 qr_action_cards.dart deletes in the same PR.
