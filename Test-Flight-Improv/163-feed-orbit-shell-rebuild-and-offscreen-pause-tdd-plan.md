## Exact Problem Statement
The Feed/Orbit shell (`feed_wired.dart`) keeps **both** panes mounted and reactive even when one is fully off-screen, and rebuilds more of itself than it needs to:

1. **navigation-hangs-2 (shell over-rebuild + un-isolated panes).** `AppShellController` (`app_shell_controller.dart:22-36`) calls the **identical** `notifyListeners()` from both `switchTo` (tab change, `:27`) and `setBackgroundPreference` (background-only change, `:35`). The sole listener `_onShellChanged` (`feed_wired.dart:2182-2195`) `setState()`s **unconditionally** (`:2190`), re-entering `build()` (`:2326`) which freshly allocates both panes — `FeedScreen` (~28 params, `:2356`) and `OrbitWired` (~40 params, `:2386`/`:2205`). The `AnimatedBuilder` (`:2400`) has a `(context, child)` builder signature (`:2402`) but **ignores `child`** and is passed **no `child:`** arg, so the panes live in the rebuilt subtree with **zero RepaintBoundary** (grep returned none): a rebuild/repaint of one pane can dirty the other.
