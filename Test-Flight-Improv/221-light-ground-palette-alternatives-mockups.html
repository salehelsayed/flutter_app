Four ways to give the light ground a spine.

The home screens — Orbit, Feed, Chat — float on a flat white today, and the content drowns in it. Below, the same three screens sit on four cohesive light grounds, each a complete color system: ground, ambient, ink, rings, node glows, pills, nav and the chat bar all tuned to one another. Every text tone is checked to WCAG AA. Compare, then pick one.

01
Ghost text. Hint & placeholder copy renders near-white — “tap to say hi” is invisible.
02
Vanishing rings. Orbit rings are so pale the structure disappears.
03
Dead glows. Node halos are weak — the “living” metaphor falls flat.
04
Muddy air. The blue/lilac blobs read like a smudge, not atmosphere.
05
Black slab. The chat input bar is stark black against white — jarring.
