Halo studies — Black · Red · White · Green 💫

Nine takes on the Halo: one radiant ring, a glowing core, a friend with an unread dot. Same four colors throughout. The first two are the refined baselines from before; the other seven push the idea — signal pulses, a comet, split and interlocking rings, and an eclipse (mknoon means hidden — the eclipse literally conceals the light).

Nine halo variations

H1 · VERDE

Halo Verde

The baseline you liked: emerald halo, glowing core, one friend with a red unread dot on deep green-black.

H2 · ROUGE

Halo Rouge

The crimson twin — red halo on wine-black, green unread dot. Striking and unusual for a messenger.

H3 · PULSE

Pulse

The halo as a signal: rings ripple outward and fade past the edge — a message radiating from you. Very "live network".

H4 · COMET

Comet

The friend node in motion — a bright head with a tail that fades around the ring. The halo gets direction and life.

H5 · DUO

Duo

One halo, two halves — emerald and crimson meeting at two white nodes. Balanced, graphic, flag-coded without shouting.

H6 · DUET

Duet

Two halos — two people — interlocking, with the shared glow and core where they meet. The most "connection" of the set.

H7 · ECLIPSE

Eclipse

mknoon = concealed. A dark disc hides the light, leaving a white crescent and a green rim — private by design. Thin outer halo + red star keep it alive.

H8 · IVORY DAY

Ivory Day

The light-mode halo: one confident flag-green ring on warm ivory, black core, red friend node ringed in white. Cleanest of the nine.

H9 · EMERALD FIELD

Emerald Field

The halo on a full flag-green field — white glowing ring, white core, red unread dot. Brightest brand-color option; unmistakable at any size.

Teal halo × blue cosmos — the original teal ring on five blue skies

H10 · CONSTELLATION SKY

Constellation Sky

Exactly what you asked for: the teal halo + pink unread dot on the Constellation card's indigo sky, with its teal and pink corner blooms.

H11 · ROYAL COSMOS

Royal Cosmos

The brightest blue: a luminous royal-indigo radial (from the Bright Cosmos card) that lifts the whole icon — the teal ring pops hardest here.

H12 · TRUE COSMIC

True Cosmic

Closest to the app itself: the in-app cosmic background (deep navy + indigo bloom bottom-left, teal bloom top-right), brightened enough to never read black.

H13 · VIOLET TWILIGHT

Violet Twilight

Blue leaning violet, with a strong pink nebula in the corner — the dreamiest of the five; the pink dot feels native to the sky.

H14 · OCEAN NEBULA

Ocean Nebula

A cyan-blue sea of a sky — tone-on-tone with the teal ring for a calm, cohesive, single-family look.
