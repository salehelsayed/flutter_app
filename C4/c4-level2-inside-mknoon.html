4What happens when a message arrives

  1. Your friend's phone sends it. If your app is open, it lands straight away — the engine takes it and passes it to the screens.
  2. If your phone was off or the app was closed, the helper server keeps it and asks the alert service to tap your phone.
  3. The alert helper wakes up, fetches the waiting message, and puts the real text into the banner you see.
  4. When you open the app, the screens ask the engine for anything still outstanding.
  5. The engine takes the keys out of the safe and unlocks the message.
  6. The screens write it into the locked notebook and show it in the chat.

5Worth knowing

  • Only the engine talks to the outside. The screens never touch the network on their own. They ask the engine, and the engine answers.
  • The safe is small on purpose. It holds a handful of secrets, not your data. Everything else goes in the locked notebook, and the key to that notebook lives in the safe.
  • The alert helper is a different program. It has its own small copy of the engine, because the app may not be running at all when an alert lands.
  • The busiest wire has the thinnest safety net. Everything the app does passes between the screens and the engine, yet that is the one connection where no test holds both ends at once, unless it runs on a real phone.
  • There is also a small share-sheet helper on iPhone, for sending a photo from another app into mknoon. It is a side door, left out to keep the drawing simple.
